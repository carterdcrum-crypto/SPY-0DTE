# SPY 0DTE — $0 breakout failure audit

**Research-only. No data purchase. No live or paper-account changes.**

## Research question

Why does an intraday **15-prior-close** SPY breakout signal
fail to predict the next ten-minute **underlying SPY** move? Can
a few **fixed, causal, predeclared** trade abstention rules reduce
false breakouts and the cumulative loss-like behavior in **signed SPY
basis points**? This is *not* a true opening-15-minute high/low range
breakout and is *not* an executable options P&L backtest.

Previous research used exactly 128 licensed SPY minute snapshot sessions.
They contain close, volume-ratio features and sampled option quotes,
**not the raw underlying OHLC highs/lows or exact observed option NBBO
events/sizes** required for an executable 0DTE options study.

## Baseline and event definition

Reuse the **unmodified nonoverlapping baseline** events generated by
`engine.free_signal_screen.screen_session`.

- Completed 1-minute SPY close at signal T breaks the maximum/minimum
  of the preceding 15 completed minute **closes**; no lookahead.
- CALL on upside, PUT on downside. Must have 30 minutes of completed
  observations and signal between 10:00–15:25 ET.
- An event enters at the **close of next completed minute** T+1,
  then evaluates signed SPY price movement through **ten more minutes**.
  This future close is a *forecast proxy*, not a simulated option buy/sell
  or an executable SPY fill. Every T+1 to T+11 minute must exist.
- Keep the baseline's rule preventing overlapping ten-minute events.
  Every filter is an abstention on an **identical preexisting baseline
  opportunity set**, not a new hand-picked signal clock.
- Fail closed for filtered candidates if 30-minute prior data continuity
  is incomplete (but preserve the original baseline event).

## Five **fixed** candidates; one parameter set, no optimization grid

| Name | Causal filter applied to the same baseline event |
| --- | --- |
| `baseline_all` | All original baseline breakout opportunities |
| `volume_plus_five_minute_trend` | At-signal SPY volume ratio >=1.25 AND signed 5-minute close change >=3 bps |
| `thirty_minute_trend_plus_efficiency` | Signed 30-minute close trend >=8 bps AND directional efficiency ratio over last 15 closes >=0.35 |
| `low_extension_plus_volume` | Distance beyond previous 15-close boundary <=8 bps (and >=0), volume ratio >=1.25 |
| `combined_conservative` | All five constraints (volume, 5m trend, 30m trend, efficiency, extension) |

The efficiency statistic is absolute net 15-minute close movement
divided by the sum of absolute minute close-to-close changes.
Features are calculated from signals and **prior** closes only; no
future close, adverse movement, option premium or holdout metric may
influence filter admission.

## Why failed? Diagnosed *after* the forecast only

For **all original baseline signals, including rejected ones**,
write `all_close_breakout_opportunities.csv` with:

- Breakout direction and distance beyond the preceding 15-close boundary.
- Contemporaneously known SPY volume ratio.
- Signed 5-minute and 30-minute price trend.
- Last 15-minute close-path efficiency (choppy or directional).
- Time bucket: 10–11 ET, 11–13:30 ET, 13:30–15:25 ET.
- **Future outcome diagnostics ONLY:** signed T+1 to T+11 bps,
  adverse/favorable **minute-close-only** excursions (not candle
  intrabar highs/lows), first-three-minute adverse movement, whether
  any of the first three post-entry minute **closes** snap back inside
  the prior close-breakout boundary.

`breakout_failure_strata.csv` compares these prespecified factors.
Any strata with fewer than 20 observations are **explicitly flagged**.
These are *post hoc failure mechanisms*, **not permission to define a
new filter and claim it passed out-of-sample.**

## Report conventions and risk

- A fixed hypothetical **2 bps per accepted SPY forecast event**
  is subtracted for proxy-friction sensitivity. This is **NOT real
  stock/option execution cost** or an option premium model. 0DTE options
  can have much larger effective costs.
- Report baseline opportunities, accepted/skipped, hit rate, mean
  signed price bps, mean after 2bp hypothetical friction, daily signed
  bps **including abstentions/no-trade sessions**, worst day and worst
  ISO week, and the largest peak-to-trough decline of the
  **additive signed bps sequence**. **This is NOT account max drawdown.**
- Bootstrap 1000 circular **5-trading-session blocks** from the
  period's per-day net signed bps vector, **paired against exactly the
  same resampled days under the no-filter baseline**. 95% intervals
  are exploratory, not predictive. Days without an event count as zero.
- All periods in `engine.free_signal_screen.PERIODS` are included:
  Apr–Jul development (82 days), Aug–Sep validation (25 days),
  Sep–Oct **previously studied** diagnostic (21 days).
  The latter is NOT an untouched test because prior experiments
  already analyzed it.
- No model is eligible for autonomous trading without positive and
  robust fresh held-out signal evidence **and real 0DTE option**
  timestamped bid/ask, liquidity, latency, fees and spread backtests.
- +100% weekly **account** returns are unmeasured and unsupported;
  no experimental SPY bps number is presented as account return.

## Interpretation guard

A filter that raises its per-trade average but rejects most trades
may *lower* day/weekly total opportunity and fail as an economic
strategy. That is why the paired **mean net bps per calendar-eligible
session**, including no-trade days, and the 5-session-block CI
vs the unfiltered baseline matter more than event hit rate.
No new dataset expense is authorized, so this diagnostic can at most
decide which failure mechanisms deserve further data collection.
